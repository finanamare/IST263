Understanding Sports Betting

Before learning about sports betting, it is important to understand different types of bets and how betting odds work.

Types of Sports Bets

Moneyline

A moneyline bet is when someone picks which team or player will win a game. The person does not need to predict the final score.

Point Spread

A point spread bet is based on how many points a team wins or loses by. The sportsbook gives one team a point advantage or disadvantage.

Over and Under

An over and under bet is when someone predicts whether the combined total score of both teams will be higher or lower than a certain number.

Parlay Betting

A parlay combines two or more bets into one. Every selection must win for the parlay to win. Parlays can offer higher payouts, but they are harder to win.

Understanding Betting Odds

Betting odds show how much money someone could win from a bet. Negative odds usually indicate a favorite, while positive odds usually indicate an underdog.

Example of Sports Betting Odds
Team Odds Meaning
Team A -150 Bet $150 to win $100 profit
Team B +200 Bet $100 to win $200 profit

These odds are examples only and do not represent actual sports betting markets.

Why Understanding Odds Matters

Understanding odds helps people recognize the risks involved in sports betting. No bet is guaranteed to win, and people can lose the money they wager.
