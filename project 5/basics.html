Understanding Sports Betting

Before learning about sports betting, it is important to understand different types of bets and how odds work.

Types of Sports Bets

Moneyline

A moneyline bet is when someone picks which team or player will win a game.

Point Spread

A point spread bet is based on how many points a team wins or loses by.

Over and Under

This bet is about whether the total score will be higher or lower than a set number.

Example of Betting Odds

Example Betting Odds
Team Odds Meaning
